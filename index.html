▦ Gridpaper

Gridpaper in 30 seconds

  1. Pick 2D Layout (websites, app screens) or 3D Scene (rooms, objects, game levels).
  2. Start from a template or click shapes in the left panel to add them.
  3. Select anything and give it a clear label and a note for Claude on the right.
  4. Press Copy image and paste it into Claude Code, then Copy description and paste that too. Or Download for Claude to get both files.

2D

Drag to move, corner squares to resize, drag empty space to pan, wheel to zoom. Arrows nudge (Shift = 100 px). Hold Alt to skip snapping. Red notes and arrows are for explaining behaviour.

3D (Blender-style)

Click to select. Drag to orbit, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. G move, R rotate, S scale. Views: 1 front, 3 right, 7 top, 5 perspective, F fit. X deletes. Use the bevel presets to round or chamfer edges.

Everywhere

Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo, Ctrl+D or Shift+D duplicate, Delete removes, Esc deselects. Your work is saved in this browser automatically; use Save to keep a file.
